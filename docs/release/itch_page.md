# itch.io 등록 안내 / itch.io page

## 올리는 법 (계정은 직접 만들어야 한다)
1. https://itch.io 가입 → Dashboard → Create new project
2. Kind of project: **HTML** (브라우저에서 바로 플레이)
3. Uploads: `builds/stoop_web.zip` 올리고 "This file will be played in the browser" 체크
4. Embed options: 1280 × 720, **Fullscreen button** 켜기, "Mobile friendly" 켜기 (터치 조작 지원)
5. SharedArrayBuffer 옵션은 **끄기** (스레드 없는 빌드라 필요 없다)
6. 윈도우판: `builds/windows/STOOP.exe`를 zip으로 묶어 추가 업로드 (Windows 체크)
7. 스크린샷·커버: `docs/skeam/` 이미지 사용 (커버 권장 630×500 → poster_600x900이나 banner를 잘라 쓰기)

상승기류로 높이를 벌고, 날개를 접어 총알처럼 내리꽂아라. 명중 순간 시간이 멈추고 깃털이 터진다.

## Description (EN)
Climb a thermal, tuck your wings and drop like a bullet. Time freezes on impact and feathers burst.
Court a mate, raise chicks, and when your time comes, continue as one of your young.

- Stoop hunting: aim for the ◇ lead mark, strike from above and behind — 250+ km/h is a PERFECT STOOP
- Hatch from the egg and learn from your mother
- Three far islands, a city that lights up at night, storms and lightning
- Random events, upgrades and plumages, and the legend of the white falcon
- Dynamic music, Korean/English, keyboard+mouse / gamepad / touch
